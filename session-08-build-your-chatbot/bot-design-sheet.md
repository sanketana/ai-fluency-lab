### ✅ The "is it sharp enough?" check

Before you turn this into a system prompt, make sure you can answer YES:

- [ ] Could a stranger guess my bot's personality from its very first reply?
- [ ] Does my bot have at least **two clear NEVER rules**?
- [ ] Do I know what my bot will say if someone asks an off-topic question?
- [ ] Did I decide how long and what style its answers are?

If any box is unchecked, your plan is still too vague. Sharpen it — that's the
real work.
