## Task 1: Build a Second Explainer

In class you built one type of explainer for one subject. Now build a **different** one.

**Rules:**
- Pick a **different subject** than what you used in class
- Pick a **different explainer type** than what you used in class
- Use a topic you're actually studying right now (upcoming test, current chapter, ongoing coursework)

**What to submit:**
1. The **Claude conversation link** (click "Share" in Claude and copy the link)
2. A **3-sentence summary**: What you built, what you changed after checking accuracy, and whether you'd use it to study

**Grading:** We're not grading the explainer's looks. We're looking at:
- Did you pick an explainer type that actually fits your topic?
- Did you verify accuracy and make corrections?
- Can you explain why you made the choices you made?

## Task 2: Exam Prep Challenge

Pick an **upcoming test or exam topic** and use AI to generate a study assessment.

**Steps:**
1. Take a photo of the relevant textbook chapter (or use a PDF if you have one)
2. Upload it to Claude
3. Use the exam quiz generator prompt (`prompt-templates/09-exam-quiz-generator.md`) to generate a 10-question quiz
4. **Actually take the quiz** — answer every question honestly
5. Get the gap analysis from Claude

**What to submit:**
1. A **screenshot** of your quiz results (score + gap analysis)
2. **2-3 sentences** answering: "Did the AI accurately identify your weak areas? How do you know?"

**Be honest:** If you guess on questions, the gap analysis won't be useful. The point is to find what you DON'T know — not to get a high score.
