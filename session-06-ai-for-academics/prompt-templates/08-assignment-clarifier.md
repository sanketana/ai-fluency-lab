# Prompt Template: Assignment Clarifier

**Purpose:** Helps students understand what an assignment is actually asking for, break down grading criteria, and plan their approach
**When to use:** When you receive an assignment brief and need to understand the requirements before starting
**Ethical boundary:** This prompt helps you UNDERSTAND the assignment — it does NOT write the assignment for you

---

## The Prompt

```
I have a school assignment and I need help understanding exactly what's expected. I do NOT want you to do the assignment for me. I want you to help me understand it so I can do it well myself.

Here is the assignment brief:
---
[PASTE YOUR FULL ASSIGNMENT BRIEF HERE]
---

Please help me with the following:

1. **Break down the requirements:** What exactly is this assignment asking me to do? List each requirement separately in simple language.

2. **Identify the grading criteria:** Based on the brief, what do you think the teacher is looking for? What would make this an excellent submission vs. an average one?

3. **Clarify confusing terms:** Are there any terms or phrases in the brief that a student might misunderstand? Explain them.

4. **Suggest a structure/approach:** What's a good way to organize my work? Give me a skeleton outline or step-by-step plan (NOT the actual content).

5. **Common mistakes to avoid:** What do students typically get wrong on this type of assignment?

6. **Questions I should ask my teacher:** Are there any ambiguities in the brief where I should get clarification?

Remember: Help me understand and plan, not write. I want to do the actual work myself.
```

---

## Customization Notes

| What to change | Why | Example |
|----------------|-----|---------|
| Assignment brief | Paste the FULL brief — every detail matters | Include word count, due date, format requirements, rubric if provided |
| Subject context | Add what subject/class this is for | "This is for Grade 10 English Literature — we're studying Shakespeare" |
| Your level | Tell AI where you stand | "I'm strong at research but weak at structuring essays" |
| Specific confusion | Highlight what's unclear to you | "I especially don't understand what 'critically analyze' means here" |

---

## What to Expect

A structured breakdown with:
- Numbered list of specific requirements extracted from the brief
- Inferred grading criteria and priorities
- Definitions of any technical/academic terms
- A skeleton outline or approach plan
- List of common pitfalls

**Important:** The AI is inferring grading criteria — it doesn't know your specific teacher. Use this as a starting checklist, then confirm with your teacher or rubric if available.

---

## Follow-Up Prompts

After getting the initial breakdown:
- "I've started working on section 2 and I'm stuck on how to approach [specific part]. Can you suggest 3 different angles I could take? Don't write it for me — just give me the angles."
- "Here's my outline so far: [paste outline]. Does it cover all the requirements you identified? What am I missing?"
- "What does 'primary sources' mean for this specific assignment? Where would I find them?"
- "My teacher said to 'critically evaluate' — what does that look like in practice for a Grade 10 essay?"
