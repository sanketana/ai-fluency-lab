# Which Artifact Should I Use? — A Student's Guide

Choosing the right artifact type is like choosing the right tool — a hammer is great for nails but terrible for screws. This guide helps you pick the best artifact for what you're trying to learn.

---

## Quick Decision Matrix

| Artifact | Best When You Need To... | Think of It As... |
|----------|-------------------------|-------------------|
| [Flashcard App](01-flashcard-app.md) | Memorize facts, terms, definitions, formulas | A smart set of index cards |
| [Interactive Quiz](02-interactive-quiz.md) | Test yourself and find knowledge gaps | A practice exam with instant feedback |
| [Concept Visualizer](03-concept-visualizer.md) | Understand how a process or system works | An animated textbook diagram you can control |
| [Simulator](04-simulator.md) | Explore "what happens if I change X?" | A virtual lab where you tweak variables |
| [Timeline](05-timeline.md) | See events in order and understand cause-effect | A visual story of how things unfolded |
| [Mindmap](06-mindmap.md) | See how ideas connect across a topic | A bird's-eye view of an entire chapter |
| [Step-by-Step Explainer](07-step-by-step-explainer.md) | Learn HOW to do something (a method or procedure) | A patient tutor walking you through each step |
| [Assignment Clarifier](08-assignment-clarifier.md) | Understand what an assignment is really asking | A decoder ring for assignment briefs |
| [Exam Quiz Generator](09-exam-quiz-generator.md) | Prepare from YOUR actual textbook/notes | A custom exam paper made from your material |

## Choosing by Study Scenario

Not sure what you're studying for? Start here.

### "I have an exam tomorrow"
1. **Exam Quiz Generator** — upload your textbook chapter, take a practice test, get a gap analysis
2. **Flashcard App** — rapid-fire revision of terms and facts you keep forgetting
3. **Interactive Quiz** — test yourself on the full chapter

### "I don't understand this concept"
1. **Concept Visualizer** — if it's a process or system (how something works)
2. **Step-by-Step Explainer** — if it's a method or procedure (how to do something)
3. **Simulator** — if it's about relationships between variables (what happens when X changes)

### "I need to revise an entire chapter"
1. **Mindmap** — get the big picture of how everything connects
2. **Flashcard App** — drill the key terms and facts
3. **Interactive Quiz** — test your overall understanding

### "I got an assignment and I'm confused"
1. **Assignment Clarifier** — understand what's being asked before you start

### "I need to remember a lot of facts"
1. **Flashcard App** — the clear winner for pure memorization
2. **Interactive Quiz** — test whether you've actually memorized them

### "I can't see how topics connect"
1. **Mindmap** — shows the big picture and relationships
2. **Timeline** — if the connection is chronological (one thing led to another)

### "I can solve simple problems but not hard ones"
1. **Step-by-Step Explainer** — learn the method properly from scratch
2. **Simulator** — build intuition for how the variables work
3. **Interactive Quiz** — practice with increasing difficulty

## Combining Artifacts for Deep Study

The most effective approach is to combine 2-3 artifacts in sequence. Here are proven combinations:

### The Understand-Then-Test Flow
> Mindmap → Concept Visualizer → Interactive Quiz

Start with the big picture (Mindmap), dive into the process you find trickiest (Concept Visualizer), then test yourself (Interactive Quiz).

### The Exam Prep Flow
> Exam Quiz Generator → Flashcard App → Exam Quiz Generator (round 2)

Take a practice test, make flashcards for what you got wrong, then retest to confirm you've improved.

### The Math/Physics Mastery Flow
> Step-by-Step Explainer → Simulator → Interactive Quiz

Learn the method (Explainer), build intuition by experimenting (Simulator), then test your problem-solving (Quiz).

### The History Deep-Dive Flow
> Timeline → Mindmap → Interactive Quiz

See events in order (Timeline), understand how themes connect (Mindmap), then test your knowledge (Quiz).

## One Important Reminder

No matter which artifact you choose — **always verify the AI's output against your textbook**. Every prompt template includes a "Quality Check" section. Use it. The goal is to learn with AI, not to blindly trust AI.
