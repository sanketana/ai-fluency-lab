How to play

  1. Train a Teachable Machine Image Project with 4 or more gestures of your choice (thumbs up, peace sign, wave, point — anything!)
  2. Export → Upload → Copy the shareable link
  3. Paste it above and click Load
  4. Aliens will fall from the sky, each labelled with one of your gestures
  5. Show the matching gesture to your camera to destroy the target alien
  6. Don't let aliens reach the bottom — you only have 3 lives!
